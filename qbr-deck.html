SEM built more pipeline but closed less of it

Paid demand held on roughly flat spend. Pipeline grew 12%, yet revenue fell 17% as conversion softened.

Highlights

  • Pipeline grew 12% to $372K on roughly flat paid spend. Cheaper clicks (CPC $22.88 to $18.58) fed more top of funnel.
  • Lead quality into sales held up: MQL to SQL rose to 43% and SQL edged up 3% even as MQL volume dipped.

Lowlights

  • Revenue fell 17% to $86K and wins dropped 60 to 53. The extra pipeline isn't closing.
  • Pipe to Revenue slid 31% to 23% and SQL to Win 17% to 15%. Conversion, not demand, is the SEM constraint this quarter.

SEO is the quarter's growth engine

Revenue up 70% and wins up 57%, with conversion improving at every step. The catch: it rode on shrinking organic reach.

Highlights

  • Revenue jumped 70% to $137K and wins 57% to 129, the largest revenue contribution of any Marketing channel.
  • Conversion improved at every step: MQL to SQL 68%, SQL to Win 20%, Pipe to Revenue 19%.

Lowlights

  • The gains came from conversion, not reach. Organic impressions fell 37% and clicks 34% (GSC); if ranking erosion continues, the lift may not hold.
  • Revenue leans on EMEA ($68K of $137K); NAMER organic is underweight at $38K.
