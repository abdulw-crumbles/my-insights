Executive summary

Deals don't fail fast. They fade.

Q2 added 32% more pipeline but only 4% more wins. The reason is not competition or price. Half of everything we lost in Q2 was pipeline that simply went cold, and that slow-death loss grew 56% in a single quarter.

The pattern

Slow death. Gone Cold and CRM Clean-up are the biggest lost buckets and sit unworked for 47 to 97 days before we give up.

It is growing

Slow-death lost pipeline rose from $275K to $430K QoQ on flat deal count. Competitive losses actually shrank.

It is worst on big deals

The higher the ACV, the slower the death. $5K+ deals take 56 days to lose, and high-ACV is half of all lost value.

Drilling into the Q2 spike

Almost the entire Q2 cold spike is one book

A single NAMER South West book, reassigned in April and never worked, was bulk-closed in June. It alone is 54% of all Q2 Gone Cold pipeline.

Q2 Gone Cold pipeline · narrowing to one book

Three facts, one conclusion

Reassigned. 39 of the 65 deals moved from a NAMER South West sales director to one AE in April. They were inherited, not originated.

Unworked. The owner's last login was mid-May. The book then sat untouched.

Bulk closed. 46 of the 65 were marked Gone Cold on a single day, 7 June. A CRM sweep, not 65 separate losses.

Where the value hides

The tail carries it. $3K+ is 17 deals, 9% of count, but $140K, 46% of all cold value. Averaging deal size hides this completely.

Mostly the one book. It holds $94K of that $140K across 10 deals, including the single $37.6K deal that went cold.

But not only. $46K across 7 deals over $3K sits outside the book, two of them $10K+. That tail is independent and ongoing, and it is what a manager review on $3K+ deals should target.

APAC SE and EMEA SE drift to 40 to 42 days. Their deals do not get worse, they get older. That ageing is where cold sets in.

The fix is a follow-up SLA by segment, not reallocation. NAMER AEs sit in the middle at 35 days with room to tighten.

Takeaways and where to act

Work the pipeline before adding to it

The diagnosis

The loss is self-inflicted

Half of Q2 lost pipeline went cold rather than to a competitor. Gone Cold and CRM Clean-up sit 47 to 97 days unworked.

It scales with deal size

Win rate falls and ageing rises as ACV grows. $5K+ deals take 56 days to lose, and high-ACV is half of all lost value.

It is a velocity gap, not a routing gap

EMEA AEs cut dead deals in 13 days. APAC and EMEA SEs drift past 40. The differentiator is follow-up cadence by segment.

Where to act

Put an SLA on post-demo follow-up

Flag any demo-delivered deal with no activity past 21 days. Force a next step or a fast disqualification. Targets the $430K slow-death bucket.

Target · halve Gone Cold pipeline

Protect high-ACV deals

Manager review on every $3K+ deal at 30 days post-demo. This is where the slow death is most expensive.

Target · lift $3K+ win rate above 17%

Borrow EMEA AE discipline

Apply the EMEA AE close cadence to APAC and EMEA SE benches. Faster decisions free capacity and cut cold losses.

Target · SE lost-deal age under 25 days
