Platform Signals
ChatGPT — volume with a ceiling

Peaked at 1,353 sessions in Nov 2025, two months after HR Tech LV. By Apr 2026 it had halved to 623/mo. OpenAI's shift toward answering queries directly rather than citing sources is reducing referral clicks industry-wide. This is not a Multiplier-specific signal, but it means ChatGPT cannot be relied on as a growing referral channel.

Gemini — the structural growth channel

47 sessions in Jun 2025 to 213 in Apr 2026 (+354%), with a 2.23% form CVR. The only LLM showing consistent month-on-month session growth. Google embedding Gemini deeper into Search makes this structurally different from other LLMs. Optimising content for Gemini citations is likely to keep compounding.

Claude — small volume, exceptional intent

449 sessions across 11 months — but a 4.23% form CVR, the highest of any LLM. One form submission for every 24 Claude-referred visitors. These are deep-research buyers already comparing vendors. Sessions also grew 2.7× in the window, from 35 to 96/mo. Worth building content specifically for Claude's citation behaviour.

Perplexity — awareness, not demand

2,084 sessions but a 0.53% CVR — 11 form submits in 11 months. Perplexity users read and move on. The content Multiplier ranks for on Perplexity appears to be informational rather than commercial intent. A different content strategy — targeting comparison and vendor selection queries — may be needed to convert this audience.

Brand Narrative · 5 Moments · Jan 2024 – Dec 2025

Five Initiatives. One Compounding Arc.

Each initiative built on the last. None was a standalone campaign. Together they shifted how the market encounters Multiplier — and the demand data reflects it.

Jul 2024
Website Refresh
World Without Limits — brand promise, ungated assets, new identity

First brand initiative. Introduced the brand philosophy and removed friction from the website. This was the foundation, not a growth moment — but it set the conditions for everything that followed.

Jan 2025
Brand Manifesto
The 'why we exist' moment

Gave the market a narrative to latch onto. SQLs climbed 20% above baseline, and pipeline jumped 39% — the first real commercial signal that brand was translating into demand quality, not just awareness.

Jun 2025
CNBC Documentary
8-minute documentary on CNBC — the credibility moment

This is the inflection point. Inbound surged to 324 contacts in June alone — from an average of under 20. People sought us out. Paid search didn't do this. The documentary did.

Jul 2025
Beyond Borders Series
4-part thought leadership series, amplified pre/during/post

Sustained amplification across all channels. The pre/during/post architecture meant the signal lasted multiple months rather than spiking and dying. Direct traffic began its sustained climb — brand recall, not retargeting.

Sep 2025
HR Tech LV + Destination Multiplier
Biggest brand event — HR Tech Las Vegas launch

The demand data shows the biggest and most durable commercial lift. SQLs hit all-time high. Pipeline in Oct–Dec 2025 ran 143% above baseline. And crucially — these numbers didn't fall back. The floor permanently rose.

SEO & Brand Authority · Ahrefs · Jan 2024 – Apr 2026

Brand Investment Built Organic Authority.

Domain Rating climbed from 50 to 68 in 18 months — a 36% improvement. Top-3 keyword positions grew 52%. The acceleration correlates directly with the brand initiative calendar, with the sharpest gains coming after the CNBC Documentary and HR Tech events.

Domain Rating & Top-3 Keywords, Jan 2024 – Apr 2026

What the authority trajectory shows
DR 50 → 68 in 18 months

Domain Rating moved 18 points in 18 months — an average of one point per month. The pace accelerated after mid-2025. Brand-driven content and the CNBC documentary generated earned backlinks that paid campaigns cannot replicate.

Top-3 keywords +52%

Rankings in positions 1–3 grew from 1,476 to 2,241. These are the positions that generate measurable click-through — not vanity rankings at position 8–10. The growth shows brand authority is converting into search visibility at the premium end of the results page.

Brand initiatives drove the lift

The four annotation lines on the chart are not decorative. Each brand initiative corresponds to a step-change in DR or keyword count. The pattern is consistent: brand investment generates external coverage, external coverage generates backlinks, backlinks build DR, DR unlocks keyword rankings. The pipeline is working.
