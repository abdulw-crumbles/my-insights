EDITORIAL NOTE · EMEA WATCH
EMEA had the highest baseline WCR (1.19%) on the old homepage and drifted to 0.87% on the new one. The drop is not statistically significant given the smaller session count, but it is the only region trending negative. Worth a follow-up cut on UK / DE / FR specifically before declaring a regional pattern.

EDITORIAL · CLOSING ARGUMENT

The New Baseline.

  • ① Conversion lift is real. WCR moved 0.48% → 0.64% (+32.6%, p = 0.06). Confidence intervals nearly separate. One to two more weeks of New HP data should clear the formal <0.05 threshold.
  • ② The lift is concentrated where it counts: new visitors (+43%, sig), desktop (+43.5%, sig), NAMER (+139%, sig). These are the cohorts that drive new pipeline.
  • ③ Engagement is structurally better. Bounce rate down 8.3 points (p < 0.001). This is the single cleanest data point in the entire report.
  • ④ Sales gets better leads. Homepage-first cohort: SQLs up +38%, Lead → MQL conversion up +4.6 pts. Same daily lead volume, materially better quality.
  • ⑤ Two watch-items, not blockers: EMEA WCR drifted -27% (not significant); Mobile WCR essentially flat. Worth a follow-up cut, not a launch reversal.
  • ⑥ This is now the baseline. Future homepage experiments compete against 0.64% WCR · 62.7% bounce · 13s on page · 37.6% Lead-to-SQL.
