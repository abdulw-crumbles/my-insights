Referrals · Partner onboarding

We approve partners fast, but few ever activate

Application to approval is healthy. The leak is activation, approved partners who never send a lead, and that is the Referrals MQL and SQL gap.

Partner funnel · 2026 to date

377 partners, Feb to Jun. The drop is after approval, not before.
Approval is fast and healthy

52% approved, 196 of 377, in a median 2.7 days from application to accept. The front of the funnel works.

Activation is low and slow

Only 43 of 196 approved partners send a lead. 153 sit dormant, and active ones take a median 36 days to first lead.

This is the Referrals demand gap

43 active partners drove 706 leads, ~16 each. Activating ~28% of the 153 dormant would double Referral leads, feeding MQL and SQL.

Takeaways and where to act

Two engines, opposite problems

Win the close on Marketing, activate the partners on Referrals.

The diagnosis
Marketing wins demand, loses the close

Funnel is on pace but revenue sits at 29% of plan. The leak is pipeline to revenue at 18% versus 23%, on smaller deals.

Referrals converts, but partners stay dormant

Deals run 1.7x larger and convert well, yet leads sit at 21 to 23% of plan. Only 43 of 196 approved partners send a lead; 153 are dormant.

One pattern repeats across both

EOR carries the revenue, GP builds pipeline it never closes, Field and Impact are all build with no close yet.

Where to act
Fix the close with Sales

Marketing does not need more MQLs. The win is conversion at the close and larger deals, a joint motion with Sales.

Target · pipe to revenue 18% → 23%
Activate the dormant partners

Approval is fast, 52% in under 3 days, so activation is the ceiling. Onboard and nudge the 153 dormant partners to a first lead.

Target · activate ~28% of dormant to double Referral leads
Reallocate paid and organic

Cut the generic high-CPC search campaigns, scale the high-intent ones, and lift commercial pages from positions 4 to 10 into the top 3.

Target · shift cut-list spend to scale list, push SEO to top 3
AI visibility: Multiplier already leads Google AI Mode at 36.5%, so brand pull in AI answers is strengthening.
Own the AI citation: turn that mention lead into cited sources. A 14-per-1,000 citation rate is the GEO gap to close.
